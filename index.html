What actually helps

No strategy changes the chance of winning; every ticket has the same odds. What you can change is how many people you would share a prize with. Many players pick birthdays (1–31), runs like 1-2-3, and neat patterns. "Avoid popular patterns" rejects tickets that use only numbers up to 31, contain three or more consecutive numbers, or form an even step sequence. In pari-mutuel games this raises your average payout if you do win.

Set a spending limit before you play and treat tickets as entertainment, not income.
