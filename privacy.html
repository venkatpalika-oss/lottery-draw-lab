Privacy Policy – Lottery Draw Lab

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Lottery Draw Lab does not collect, store on any server, sell or share personal data.

Data you enter

Draw history you paste or import is processed only on your device and saved in your device's browser storage so it is there next time you open the app. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Clearing the app's data removes it.

No accounts, tracking or ads

The app has no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking. The app loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of a normal web request.

Gambling

The app does not sell lottery tickets or accept payments. It provides statistics only and cannot predict lottery results. It is intended for users aged 18 and over.
