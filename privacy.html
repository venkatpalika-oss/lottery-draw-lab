Privacy Policy – Lottery Draw Lab

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Lottery Draw Lab does not collect, store on any server, sell or share personal data.

Data you enter

Everything you enter stays on your device: draw history you paste or import, number sets you save for comparison, results you add, and any spending-log entries and limit you set. It is saved in the app's local storage so it is there next time you open the app, and it is never sent to us or to anyone else. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it.

No accounts, tracking or ads

The app has no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking. All fonts and files are served with the app itself; the app does not contact any third-party service.

Gambling

The app does not sell lottery tickets, place entries, accept bets, take payments or link to lottery operators. It provides statistics and probability information only and cannot predict lottery results. The spending log only stores figures you type in. The app is intended for users aged 18 and over.
